# Pay Ledger Pro: downloads

Official downloads for **Pay Ledger Pro** by [NavaSetu Initiatives](https://navasetu.online/pay-ledger-pro).

Track your pay, DO 2 Orders and DSOPF from your PCDA(O) pay slips. Privately, on your own computer.

## Download
Get the latest version from **[Releases](../../releases/latest)**:

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Mac with Apple chip (M1, M2, M3, M4) | `PayLedgerPro-Mac-AppleChip.dmg` |
| Mac with Intel processor | `PayLedgerPro-Mac-Intel.dmg` |
| Windows 10 / 11 | `PayLedgerPro-Windows-Setup.exe` |

The download is free and opens a **full demo with sample data**.
To use it with your own pay slips, buy a licence at **[navasetu.online/pay-ledger-pro](https://navasetu.online/pay-ledger-pro)**.

## Installing
- **Mac:** see [INSTALL-mac.txt](INSTALL-mac.txt). The first time, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → *Open Anyway*.
- **Windows:** see [INSTALL-windows.txt](INSTALL-windows.txt). If you see "Windows protected your PC", click *More info* → *Run anyway*.

## Privacy
Pay Ledger Pro runs only on your computer. Your pay slips and figures are never uploaded.
