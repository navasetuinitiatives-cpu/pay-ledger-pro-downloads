PAY LEDGER PRO: INSTALLING ON WINDOWS

1. Double-click the setup file you downloaded.

2. Windows may show a blue box saying "Windows protected your PC".
   This is because the installer is not signed with a paid certificate.
   - Click "More info".
   - Click "Run anyway".

3. Follow the installer. No administrator rights are needed.

4. Pay Ledger Pro opens in your web browser at http://127.0.0.1:8770.
   It runs only on your computer. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

5. While it is running, a Pay Ledger Pro icon sits in the system tray
   (near the clock; click the ^ arrow if hidden). Click it to open the app,
   or right-click > Quit to close it.
   To open it again later, use the Start menu: Pay Ledger Pro.

Your payslip password is kept in Windows Credential Manager, never in a file.
